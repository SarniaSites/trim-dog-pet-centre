Down the aisles

We sell most leading dog, cat and small animal foods, and the bits that go with them. Pick an aisle to see what’s on the shelves.

Dogs

Dry food, tins and trays from the leading brands, including Omni, Natures Menu and Wilsons, plus raw nuggets, minces and 60/40 blocks in the freezers. Treats, coats and grooming kit sit around them.

  • Air-dried and meaty treats and chews
  • Frozen treats for hot days
  • Dog coats
  • Bandanas and bow ties, made locally by Michelle
  • Clippers, scissors, nail clippers and dog boots
  • Shampoos, conditioners, anti-tangle and colognes
  • Cooling mats and sprinklers in summer
Call to check a brand or bag size

Cats

A full aisle of wet and dry cat food from most of the leading brands. If you feed something we don’t usually keep, ask and we’ll see if we can order it in.

  • Pouches, tins and trays
  • Dry food in a choice of bag sizes
  • Treats
Call to check a brand or bag size

Small animals

We often have guinea pigs in the shop, and everything that goes with them: food, hay, bedding, treats and somewhere to live. Ring to ask who’s in.

  • Guinea pigs
  • Small animal food and treats
  • Bedding and hay
  • Cages, hutches and folding runs
Call to ask about guinea pigs

Birds

Seed, seed treats, toys and cages for pet birds. For the garden there’s wild bird seed and a wall of feeders.

  • Bird seed and seed treats
  • Toys and cages
  • Wild bird seed
  • Feeders
Call to check what’s in

Hutches & kennels

Wooden kennels, hutches, chicken houses and folding runs. Most come flat-packed, so they fit in the car and go together at home.

  • Dog kennels, also good as a tortoise hut at night
  • Hutches and cages
  • Chicken houses
  • Folding runs

Ordering by phone

Know what you need? Ring it through and we’ll have it ready to collect.

  1. 1

    Ring 01481 255555

    Tell us what you need, the brand and the bag size.

  2. 2

    We pick it off the shelf

    Your order is bagged up behind the counter. If it isn’t in stock, we’ll suggest an alternative or try to order it in.

  3. 3

    Collect from the kerb

    Pull up on Pleinheaume Road and we’ll bring it out. Ask about delivery when you call.
