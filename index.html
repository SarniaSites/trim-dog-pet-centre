What’s on the shelves

Most leading dog, cat and small animal foods, plus the treats, bedding and kit that go with them. Pick an aisle and add what you need to your list.

Wet, dry and frozen food from the leading brands, in a choice of bag sizes. The Omni range covers skin, calm and joints, in wet and dry.

  • Wet dog food
  • Omni wet or dry
  • Meaty treats and chews
  • Air-dried snacks

Frozen and raw

Raw nuggets, minces and blocks, topped up regularly. Natures Menu, puppy mixes, and frozen treats for hot days.

  • 60/40 beef nuggets
  • 60/40 blocks
  • Frozen minces
  • Natures Menu
  • Frozen treats

Cats

  • Wet cat food
  • Dry cat food
  • Cat treats

  • Treats

At the counter

The things we do besides filling the shelves.

Tags engraved while you wait

Pick a tag from the board, bones, hearts, paws or plain discs, tell us the name and number, and we’ll engrave it there and then.

Ordering in

If it isn’t on the shelf, we’ll try to order it in or suggest something similar.

Kerbside collection

Ring ahead with your order and we’ll bring it out to the car on Pleinheaume Road.

Made on the island

Bandanas and bow ties sewn locally by Michelle, to fit any size of dog.
